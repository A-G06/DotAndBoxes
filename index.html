How to Play

Classic mathematical game of strategy and tactics.

  • Core Objective: Complete more 1x1 boxes than your opponent on a 5x5 grid (6x6 dots).
  • Turn Flow: Click, tap, or drag across any unplaced line segment between adjacent dots.
  • Box Capture: When your placed line completes the 4th side of a box, you capture it, score 1 point, and earn an Extra Turn.
  • Chain Combos: Capturing multiple boxes in sequence builds a chain combo!
  • Double-Cross Strategy: In expert play, deliberately forfeiting the last 2 boxes in a long chain forces your opponent to open the next chain for you!
